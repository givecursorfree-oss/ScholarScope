How ScholarScope works

Three confident steps to a live publishing brief — APC, open access, subjects, free impact metrics, and verify links — without jumping between five tabs.

  1. Install ScholarScope

    Download ScholarScope-extension.zip, unzip once, open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode, then Load unpacked. Pin the extension to your toolbar.

    Open the full install guide →

  2. Open a journal page — or search manually

    Browse SciMAGO, DOAJ, major publishers, PubMed, or Google Scholar. ScholarScope detects ISSN or title on the page. If the page has no ISSN, open the popup and search by title or ISSN.

  3. Read the brief and decide

    Review live fees (page → DOAJ → OpenAlex), OA and Creative Commons licenses, subjects, free OpenAlex metrics (not Clarivate IF), indexing checks, and one-click links to confirm SJR on SCImago.

What you get in the brief

  • Article processing charges and other listed publishing fees
  • Open access status and license chips from DOAJ when indexed
  • Subjects and OpenAlex topics for field fit
  • 2-year mean citedness, h-index, and i10 — labeled honestly
  • DOAJ / MEDLINE checks plus verify links for SJR, Scopus, Scholar, ISSN
