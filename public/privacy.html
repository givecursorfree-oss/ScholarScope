Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026 · Applies to the ScholarScope website and Chrome/Edge extension.

Summary

ScholarScope helps researchers decide where to publish with a live journal brief (APC, open access, subjects, free impact metrics, and verify links). The core product does not require an account. We do not sell your data.

What the extension reads

  • On supported journal pages, ScholarScope may read page text needed to detect a journal title, ISSN, and any listed publishing fees.
  • Lookups use public research APIs and pages (including DOAJ, OpenAlex, and NLM/PubMed Catalog) to build the brief.
  • Fee amounts can change — always confirm the final APC on the publisher’s site before you pay or submit.

What we store locally

  • Display-currency preference may be saved in Chrome storage on your device so APC amounts can be shown in your chosen currency.
  • Brief results may be cached briefly in memory/session to avoid repeat network calls (about 30 minutes).
  • We do not operate a ScholarScope account database for the core brief.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not display Clarivate Journal Impact Factor or scrape JCR ranks.
  • We do not rewrite host pages; the brief opens in the extension UI.

Website

This marketing site hosts product information, the install guide, and the downloadable extension package. Standard server logs may record technical request data (for example IP address and user agent) as operated by your hosting provider.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support channel listed with your Chrome Web Store listing, or the contact method published on this site when available.
