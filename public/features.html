Built for researchers choosing where to publish

ScholarScope is a free Chrome extension that builds a live publishing brief from trusted open sources — not fabricated ranks, not Clarivate IF.

Real APC & fees

Prefer fees shown on the publisher page, then DOAJ, then OpenAlex — USD, EUR, or GBP when listed. Confirm the final amount on the publisher site before you pay or submit.

Open Access + license

See OA status and Creative Commons licenses from DOAJ in one glance when the journal is indexed.

Themes & subjects

Subject terms and OpenAlex topics so you can check whether the venue fits your field before you format a manuscript.

Free impact metrics

OpenAlex 2-year mean citedness, h-index, and i10 — clearly labeled as not Clarivate Journal Impact Factor.

Indexing & verify

DOAJ and MEDLINE/PubMed checks where available, plus one-click links to confirm SJR on SCImago, Scopus, Scholar, and ISSN.

Works where you search

Auto-detect on SciMAGO, DOAJ, major publishers, PubMed, and Google Scholar — or search by ISSN / title in the popup anytime.

What ScholarScope does not do

  • It does not display Clarivate Journal Impact Factor or scrape JCR
  • It does not invent official SJR or Scopus ranks
  • It is not a manuscript editor or submission service
  • It does not rewrite host pages — the brief opens in the extension UI
